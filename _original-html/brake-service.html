Warning signs

Is it time for a brake check?

Customers usually call us after ignoring a problem for too long. If you notice any of these, don’t wait.

Squealing or squeaking

A high-pitched noise when you brake is often the first sign pads are wearing thin.

Grinding or scraping

Metal-on-metal sounds need prompt attention. Don’t wait.

Soft or spongy pedal

If the pedal sinks or feels different, have the system checked right away.

Pulling to one side

Your vehicle drifting when you brake can point to uneven brake wear or a stuck component.

Vibration when stopping

A shaking pedal or steering wheel can mean warped or worn rotors.

Brake warning light

A dashboard light is your vehicle asking for an inspection.
